# Professional quality gates

Automatic gates prevent internally inconsistent concept delivery; they do not establish local-code compliance.

Every finding records stable issue ID, rule/version, severity, affected nodes/levels/requirements/evidence, reproducible measurement, threshold and source, fix, professional-verification flag, and first/latest revision. Threshold sources distinguish user requirements, supplied jurisdiction rules, product concept defaults, and comfort guidance.

## Blocking automatic checks

- Project schema, IDs, references, units, capacity, Pascal engine, ownership, and scene hash.
- Evidence classification and contradictory calibration. No scale is a visible concept warning.
- Level and room polygon self-intersection or degeneration, wall/footprint mismatch, and invalid opening ownership.
- Door/window cutouts outside walls, conflicting openings, furniture OBB collision, room escape, wall intersection, opening obstruction, and operating/maintenance clearance.
- 0.25 m walkable-grid reachability from the entry to required rooms plus widest-path bottleneck reporting against a sourced 0.75 m concept default. Door gaps are traversable; window gaps, walls, and rotated furniture remain blocked.
- Stair connection, conceptual width/headroom, void guardrail coverage, and multi-level continuity.
- Unresolved or untraceable `must` requirements.
- Missing room/item material intent, known wet-area contradiction, target budget without complete scope/category/confidence allocation, known schedule without phases, and unsupported scene capacity.
- High-risk scope without matching professional verification.
- Agent identity, delivery version and engine provenance, CSP, self-contained assets, redacted evidence, privacy fields, loopback/development paths, size, and offline fallback.

Warnings expose unknown scale, explicit blocked requirements, unverified wet-area material, unknown budget, and concept allocation beyond the target. Warnings remain in the Page; they are not silently waived.

`brief.qualityThresholds.minimumPassageWidthMetres` may replace the 0.75 m fallback only with an explicit `user-requirement`, `jurisdiction-rule`, `product-concept-default`, or `comfort-guidance` source and a visible reference when applicable.

## Human professional review

Never automatically pass load-bearing walls, structural openings, slab/stair loads, gas/flue work, waterproofing/drainage, electrical capacity/circuits, fire safety/egress, hidden services, exact construction dimensions, product batch/environmental testing, permits, or jurisdiction-specific rules. Record the owner, evidence, status, and hold point for the qualified professional.

Page generation requires zero automatic blockers. Required on-site/professional checks may remain pending only when they are explicitly recorded and displayed as limitations.
